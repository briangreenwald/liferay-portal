Adding a custom domain

Make your environment accessible under your own domain (e.g. app.yourcompany.com) in three steps.

  1. Declare the domain in your liferay.yaml. Add it under networks.domains: 
    networks:
  domains:
    - app.yourcompany.com
    After the deploy, the domain appears in the table above within a few minutes.
  2. Create two CNAME records at your DNS provider. One points your domain to the environment we provisioned, the other allows the platform to issue and renew the SSL certificate automatically: 
    app.yourcompany.com.                  CNAME   liferay-${environment}.${dns_zone}.
_acme-challenge.app.yourcompany.com.  CNAME   _acme-challenge.app.yourcompany.com.${dns_zone}.
    Replace app.yourcompany.com with your own domain. The targets above already match the ${environment} environment selected at the top.
  3. Wait a few minutes. Once both records propagate, your domain becomes accessible over HTTPS — no further action required.
⚠️ Heads up: until both CNAMEs are in place, the domain shows up in the table above but HTTPS will not work yet.
